# Family guide

How to use Medicine Reminder day to day. You use the **family app** (any phone or computer, at the address your
family set up). Thatha uses **his phone**, which rings by itself: he never needs to open anything.

> The app never suggests doses or checks drug safety. **Confirm medicines and food timing with the doctor.**

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Today for each person: what was taken, what is ringing now, what was missed. Missed doses show as a red banner at the top. |
| **Medicines** | Every medicine and its times, per person. Edit or pause times here. |
| **Routine** | Calm daily reminders (water, walk, meals) and, if turned on, period dates. |
| **Family** | The people you remind, their phones, and the family members who get alerts. |

## First time

1. **Create your family**, or **join** one with an invite code from a relative.
2. **Family → + Add a person to remind.** Choose the language the voice speaks. Add his phone number (for the
   *Call* button) and the family number his phone should call.
3. **Set up phone** (next to his name): follow [the phone checks](android-phone-test.md). Do this once, holding his
   phone.
4. **Turn on alerts** on your own phone (the card on Home). On iPhone, first *Share → Add to Home Screen* and
   open the app from there.

## Adding a medicine

**Home → + Add a medicine** (or the Medicines tab).

- **Photo of tablet:** take a clear photo. It appears on his alarm screen.
- **Medicine name:** as written on the strip (e.g. Amlodipine 5 mg). **What the voice calls it:** the name he knows,
  in his language (e.g. బీపీ మాత్ర).
- **Voice preview** shows exactly what will be spoken. Tap **Listen** if your phone has that voice.
- **Time**, **Repeat** (every day or chosen days) and **With food**. Add more times with **+ Add another time**.
- **Alarm behaviour:** first reminder (5 min before), repeat every (5 min), stop automatically after (30 min).
- Tap **Save and send to his phone**.

## When a dose is missed

You see a red banner on Home (and an alert on your phone if alerts are on). Open it:

- **Call** him.
- **Play the reminder again:** his phone rings that medicine now (needs push to be set up).
- **Already taken? Mark as taken:** if he took it but didn't tap the button.
- **This week** shows each day at a glance (✓ all taken, ! something missed).

His phone keeps working without internet. If he taps *Taken* while offline, the family app updates when his phone
is back online.

## Routine and period dates

- **Routine → + Add:** time, days, what it is ("Drink a glass of water") and, optionally, the words the voice should
  say in his language ("నీళ్ళు తాగండి"). These ring softly with an OK button and are not tracked.
- **Period dates** (turn on in the person's settings): tap **Period started today** when it starts. The card shows
  the next expected date and her phone reminds her by voice a few days before. **Dates are estimates and not medical
  advice.**

## Who can do what

- **Can edit everything** (editors): change medicines, people, routine and settings; invite family; unlink phones.
- **Missed-dose alerts** (viewers): see everything, get alerts, mark a dose as taken, record a period start.

Invite a relative: **Family → Invite a family member**, choose what they can do, and share the code. Codes work
once and expire after 24 hours.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| His alarm did not ring | On his phone: *Phone setup* — every row should be green. On Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo also allow **Autostart**. Ring a test alarm. |
| It rang but did not speak | *Phone setup → Telugu voice → Install voice*, then test again. |
| A new medicine did not reach his phone | Open the app on his phone once (it syncs). Set up push so this happens by itself. |
| "This phone is no longer linked" on his phone | Someone unlinked it. Make a new family code and pair again. |
| The family app says "You are offline" | Changes are saved when the internet is back. His alarms keep ringing meanwhile. |
